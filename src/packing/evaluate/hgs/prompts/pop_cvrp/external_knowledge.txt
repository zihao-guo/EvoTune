## DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE: HGS POPULATION PARENT SELECTION

`Population.select(...)` chooses two parents before crossover:
1. Updates biased fitness with `self._update_fitness(cost_evaluator)`.
2. Selects parents from `self._feas` and `self._infeas`.
3. Uses `self._op(parent_a, parent_b)` to measure pairwise diversity.
4. Returns `(first, second)` to the existing crossover and local-search pipeline.

### Key Optimization Areas to Consider:

- Balancing low `item.fitness` with diversity inside `[lb_diversity, ub_diversity]`.

- Robust fallbacks when diversity bounds or subpopulation balance are poor.
